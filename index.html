About me

I am a Level 2 Software Engineer at Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh and a 2024 Computer Science and Engineering graduate from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST). My work and research span intelligent sensing, autonomous systems, computer vision and AI for healthcare.

At Samsung, I contribute to application-level functionality, reliability and cross-device compatibility for Samsung Health Monitor workflows across smartphones, Galaxy Watch and Galaxy Ring in a regulated Software as a Medical Device environment. My academic work includes thoracic disease detection, assistive robot navigation and autonomous mobile robotics.
