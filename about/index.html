About me

I am a Level 2 Software Engineer at Samsung R&D Institute and a 2024 Computer Science and Engineering graduate from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST).

My work and research span Robotics and AI for healthcare. Expanding to autonomous navigation, computer vision, and wearable sensing, I aim to contribute to intelligent systems that enhance human well-being. My academic and professional journey reflects a commitment to innovation, problem-solving, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
